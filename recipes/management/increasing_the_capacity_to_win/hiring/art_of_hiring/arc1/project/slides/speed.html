Startup vibes.

MOVE SLOW, THEN FAST

“Where are you in YOUR process?”

MISSIONARY ZEEL

“What do you need to commit?”

NO PRESSURE SELLING

“This is an important decision for you — and us. Take the weekend and let us know Monday.”

Speed matters. We lose candidates when we move slowly. One candidate never even got to the onsite. Another was held back for weeks, then took a competing offer. We can control our speed. How do we create a sense of urgency on the candidate side? If we succeed, we can control their speed to some degree too. We never want to lie ("I only have 2 roles open"). Move slow, then fast: we lose if they think about it for months; schedule onsites when they are ready to decide. Missionary zeal: we expect people to make quick decisions on incomplete information, and that includes the interview. No pressure selling: we don't take important decisions lightly; give people the space to think and decide, without any fake urgency.
