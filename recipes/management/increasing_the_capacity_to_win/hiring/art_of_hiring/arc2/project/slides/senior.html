Senior isn’t “knows what I know.”

WRONG BAR

“Do they know what I know?”

Knowledge comes from experience. Yours and theirs aren’t comparable.

RIGHT BAR

“What should they know, given their path?”

Read the résumé, then test what that experience should have taught them.

LOOK FOR

Judgment

Good questions

Improvisation
