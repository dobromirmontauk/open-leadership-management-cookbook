L4, Magnet. How do you attract talent? Pro tip: you don't sell them. Good: able to demonstrate agility and depth in areas the candidate values (strength attracts strength). Better: able to impress the candidate: clearly better, in a non-threatening way, in key areas where the candidate wants to grow. Best: able to demonstrate how you personally can grow the candidate in a way the candidate values ("we can grow you"). No humble brags: be comfortable in your strengths and honest in your weaknesses.
