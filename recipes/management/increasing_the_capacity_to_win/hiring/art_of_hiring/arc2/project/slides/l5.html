L5, Landing. How do you land 10x talent? If it's about the compensation, you've already lost. Good: able to deeply connect with the candidate in a way that is meaningful to them. Better: able to pitch the value of the role/team/org/company in a way that has emotional impact. Best: able to show them why they would win here vs. other options, and have it resonate. We only live once, and work is a LOT of hours: show them your AWESOME self.
