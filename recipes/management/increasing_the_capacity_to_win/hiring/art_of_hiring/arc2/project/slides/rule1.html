Rule #1: be interested. Take responsibility for being interested: create connection, leave distractions outside the room (turn off Slack and Claude!), lower barriers to effective communication, make the candidate want to expand on details. Look for: what motivates them, what demotivates them, excitement and interest points, fear and pain points, their level of technical, personal and social skill, what they are proud of, why they took a role, why they left it. Employ active listening and curiosity: "what?", "how?", "tell me more?"
