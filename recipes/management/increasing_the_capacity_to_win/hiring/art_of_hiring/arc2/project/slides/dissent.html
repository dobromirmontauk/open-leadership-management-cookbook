From our own retro of 26 hires. When one interviewer raised a specific concern about fit or ownership, it usually came true. Doubts about speed or coding mostly didn't. A Strong Yes on technical skill didn't predict reliability. Leadership desire was never probed. Fix: any specific dissent gets a written rebuttal before an offer goes out.
