[ L1 · Competence ]
Senior isn’t “knows what I know.”
WRONG BAR
“Do they know what I know?”
Knowledge comes from experience. Yours and theirs aren’t comparable.
RIGHT BAR
“What should they know, given their path?”
Read the résumé, then test what that experience should have taught them.
LOOK FOR
Judgment
Good questions
Improvisation

[ L3 · Fit ]
What unlocks their potential HERE?
L1
L2
L3
L4
L5
A mix of alignment, role requirements and cultural fit.
GOOD
Understands their goals, the environment they need and any culture issues, and believes all three work here.
BETTER
Can lay out a plan (projects, timing) that would unlock their potential here.
BEST
Shows how similar people executed that plan and got the results here.
Copyright Dobromir Montauk, 2026
[ 11 ]
[ L3 · Fit ]
Your problem is their opportunity.
FIRES FORGE PEOPLE
The hard team can be the growth role.
Show them the fire, eyes open. Wrong people run away; the right ones get excited.
BELIEVE IN THEM
Maybe no one ever did.
Explain why the job will be hard, and why THEY will succeed.
BE CONCRETE
Training starts in the interview.
Give feedback, show our onboarding process, discuss how they would solve our problems.
Copyright Dobromir Montauk, 2026

Pro tip: you don’t sell them.

[ 18 ]
[ Rule #2 ]
Be an advocate.
 
GOOD FOR US
BAD FOR US
GOOD FOR THEM
Great employee.
They get fired when we figure it out.
BAD FOR THEM
They leave when they figure it out, or stay for the wrong reason.
Nobody wins.
Fit is established jointly.
FOR THEM
Is this the right next stage of their career? What do they want, and how will they prioritize?
DON’T GO NATIVE
You represent the company. Every candidate leaves an evangelist.
Copyright Dobromir Montauk, 2026

[ Arc 2 recap ]
Five levels, how to do each.
L1
Competence
Adapt to the candidate: standard questions, live problems, or their past work.
L2
Potential
Find repeated high-potential behavior, then probe for the blind spots.
L3
Fit
Lay out the plan, projects and timing, that unlocks them here.
L4
Magnet
Interview on the real work, and show how you’ll grow them.
L5
Landing
Find what moves them, and show them the path ahead.
